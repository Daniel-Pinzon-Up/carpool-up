# Authorisation for the processing of personal data

> Initial draft pending review by the legal office of the Universidad de Pamplona. In case of any discrepancy, the Spanish version prevails.

In compliance with Colombian **Law 1581 of 2012** and its regulatory decrees, by signing up you give prior, express and informed authorisation for the processing of your personal data under the following terms.

## 1. Data collected

- Identification and contact: name, institutional email, programme or department, sex and an optional profile photo.
- Documents: student ID or institutional certificate and, for drivers, driving licence and vehicle details.
- Use of the service: trips, bookings, ratings, chat messages and the driver’s location while a trip is in progress.
- Trusted contacts that you register voluntarily.

## 2. Purposes

- Verify that you belong to the university community.
- Allow you to publish, search for and book trips, and to communicate with the other trip participants.
- Trip safety: share link, trusted contacts and emergency handling.
- Aggregated statistics to improve the service.

## 3. Sensitive data and visibility

- Your email, phone number, student ID and licence are **never** shown to other users.
- The vehicle plate and the driver’s payment note are only shown to passengers with an accepted booking.
- The live location is only shared during the trip, with its participants or with whoever receives the temporary link.
- Chat messages are deleted after the published retention period.

## 4. Rights of the data subject

You can access, update and correct your data from your profile, request proof of this authorisation, revoke it and delete your account from your profile. When the account is deleted, your personal data is anonymised, your documents and trusted contacts are erased, and only the operational records of trips are kept (without data that identifies you).

## 5. Data controller

Universidad de Pamplona, Pamplona, Norte de Santander. The service channels for enquiries and complaints will be published in the final version of this document.
