# Terms and conditions of use

> Initial draft pending review by the legal office of the Universidad de Pamplona. In case of any discrepancy, the Spanish version prevails.

## 1. Purpose

Carpool UP (Carpool Universitario) is a platform for students, teaching staff and administrative staff of the Universidad de Pamplona to share trips by car or motorcycle within the municipality of Pamplona, Norte de Santander.

## 2. Who can use it

- Only people with an institutional email ending in `.edu.co` and a valid student ID or institutional certificate approved by an administrator.
- The student ID or certificate must be revalidated every academic semester within the published deadline.
- Each person has a single account and is responsible for the information they register.

## 3. Non-commercial nature

- The amount per seat is a **contribution to cover the costs** of the trip (fuel and wear), not a payment for a transport service or a commercial income.
- The platform does not process payments. Money is handed over in cash or by direct transfer between people. The ticket issued by the application is for information only.
- If the administration defines it, the application suggests an amount based on distance and applies a maximum per seat.

## 4. Driver obligations

- Hold a valid driving licence and upload it to the platform for approval.
- Declare that the vehicle has valid SOAT insurance and technical inspection and complies with traffic rules. The platform does not validate these documents; the driver is responsible for them.
- Respect the **pico y placa** restriction in Pamplona. Trips on restricted dates are cancelled automatically.
- Do not offer more seats than the vehicle allows, and do not carry passengers without a seat belt or helmet as applicable.

## 5. Passenger obligations

- Arrive on time at the pick-up point and cancel with the notice shown in the application.
- Respect the trip rules published by the driver.
- Repeated no-shows lead to a temporary ban on making bookings.

## 6. Conduct and safety

- Respectful behaviour during the trip and in the chat. The administration may hide content and suspend accounts that break these rules.
- The application offers a link to share the trip, trusted contacts and an emergency button. In an emergency, call **123**.

## 7. Limitation of liability

The Universidad de Pamplona and the platform make it easier for members of the university community to get in touch. They are not a transport company and are not liable for accidents, damage, losses or breaches between users, without prejudice to what Colombian law establishes.

## 8. Changes

When the current version of these terms changes, you will be asked to accept it again before continuing to use the platform.
